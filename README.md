# Chocolate Chip Cookies

Classic bakery-style chocolate chip cookies: crisp at the edge, chewy in the middle,
with pools of melted chocolate throughout.

- **Prep time:** 15 minutes
- **Chill time:** 30 minutes (optional, but recommended)
- **Bake time:** 10-12 minutes per tray
- **Yield:** 24 cookies (about 45 g of dough each)

## Ingredients

### Dry

| Ingredient | Metric | US customary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plain (all-purpose) flour | 280 g | 2 1/4 cups |
| Baking soda (bicarbonate of soda) | 6 g | 1 tsp |
| Fine sea salt | 5 g | 3/4 tsp |

### Wet

| Ingredient | Metric | US customary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unsalted butter, softened | 225 g | 1 cup (2 sticks) |
| Light brown sugar, packed | 200 g | 1 cup |
| Granulated sugar | 100 g | 1/2 cup |
| Large eggs, room temperature | 2 | 2 |
| Vanilla extract | 10 ml | 2 tsp |

### Chocolate

| Ingredient | Metric | US customary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Semi-sweet chips or chopped chocolate, 55-70% cocoa | 340 g | 2 cups |
| Flaky sea salt, for finishing (optional) | to taste | to taste |

## Equipment

- Two large baking sheets
- Parchment paper or silicone baking mats
- Stand mixer with paddle attachment, or a hand mixer and a large bowl
- Wire cooling rack
- Kitchen scale (recommended for consistent results)

## Method

1. **Prepare the trays.** Line two baking sheets with parchment paper. Do not grease
   them; greased trays make the cookies spread too far.
2. **Whisk the dry ingredients.** In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking
   soda and fine sea salt until evenly combined. Set aside.
3. **Cream the butter and sugars.** Beat the softened butter with both sugars on
   medium speed for 3-4 minutes, until pale and fluffy. Scrape down the bowl once
   or twice so everything is incorporated.
4. **Add the eggs and vanilla.** Beat in the eggs one at a time, mixing until each
   disappears before adding the next, then mix in the vanilla. The batter
   should look smooth and glossy.
5. **Combine.** Add the dry ingredients in two additions on the lowest speed, mixing
   only until the last streaks of flour vanish. Overmixing at this stage develops
   gluten and makes the cookies tough.
6. **Fold in the chocolate.** Add the chocolate chips and fold them through
   with a spatula so they are distributed evenly.
7. **Chill (recommended).** Cover the dough and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes,
   or up to 72 hours. Chilling firms the fat, limits spreading and deepens the
   caramel flavour.
8. **Heat the oven.** Preheat to **190 C / 375 F** (170 C fan). Position a rack in
   the centre of the oven.
9. **Shape.** Scoop 45 g balls of dough (about 2 tablespoons) and space them at least
   5 cm / 2 inches apart, 12 per tray. Do not flatten them.
10. **Bake.** Bake one tray at a time for **10-12 minutes**, until the edges
    are set and golden but the centres still look slightly underdone and puffy.
11. **Rest, then cool.** Sprinkle with flaky salt if using. Leave the cookies
    on the hot tray for 5 minutes to finish setting, then transfer to a wire
    rack to cool.
12. **Repeat** with the remaining dough, using a cooled tray each time.

## Tips

- **Weigh the flour.** A packed cup can hold 20% more flour than a spooned one, which
  is the most common cause of dry, cakey cookies.
- **Room-temperature eggs** emulsify into the creamed butter far more readily than
  cold ones, giving a finer crumb.
- **Chopped chocolate over chips.** Chips hold their shape by design; a chopped bar
  gives you the puddles and thin shards that make each bite different.
- **Bang the tray.** For rippled, bakery-style edges, lift the tray 10 cm and
  drop it onto the oven rack once or twice in the final 3 minutes of baking.
- **Underbake slightly.** The cookies keep cooking on the hot tray. Pull them
  when the centres still look soft; they firm up as they cool.

## Variations

- **Brown butter:** Melt the butter until the milk solids turn nut-brown, cool
  it until solid but soft again, then proceed as written. Adds a toffee note.
- **Double chocolate:** Replace 30 g of the flour with 30 g of cocoa powder.
- **Nutty:** Fold in 100 g of toasted, roughly chopped walnuts or pecans with the
  chocolate.
- **Oatmeal:** Swap 80 g of the flour for 100 g of rolled oats for a chewier cookie.

## Storage

- **Baked cookies:** Airtight container at room temperature for up to 4 days.
  A slice of bread in the container keeps them soft.
- **Raw dough:** Refrigerate for up to 72 hours, or freeze the shaped balls on
  a tray, then bag them for up to 3 months. Bake from frozen, adding 2 minutes.
- **Reheat:** 10 seconds in a microwave, or 4 minutes in a 160 C / 320 F oven, restores
  the just-baked texture.
